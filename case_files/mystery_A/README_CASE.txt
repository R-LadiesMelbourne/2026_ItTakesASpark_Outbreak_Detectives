SHARED OUTBREAK CASE – FACILITATOR NOTES

EXPECTED FINAL INTERPRETATION
• P01–P28: main Group A Streptococcus (Strep A) school outbreak (28 cases).
• P29–P30: separate Staphylococcus aureus food-poisoning event after eating chicken wraps.
• P31–P36: remain well.

MAIN OUTBREAK CLUES
• Sore throat and fever dominate the symptom profile.
• Cough is uncommon.
• Symptom onset is mostly 1.5–5 days.
• Most cases share classroom/camp contacts.
• Six sequenced outbreak samples cluster tightly with REF_STREP_A.

PHYLOGENETIC TREE DESIGN
• REF_STREP_C is deliberately placed near REF_STREP_A so the tree is not completely trivial.
• P29 and P30 cluster separately with REF_STAPH_AUREUS.

CONTACT NETWORK DESIGN
• One large connected school outbreak component.
• P29–P30 form their own disconnected pair.
• Five well students have brief contact links to the main outbreak component.
• P36 is disconnected.

DISCUSSION
Why did six students remain well despite contact? Possible explanations include differences in intensity/duration
of exposure, timing, host susceptibility, immunity, behaviour, or chance.

IMPORTANT

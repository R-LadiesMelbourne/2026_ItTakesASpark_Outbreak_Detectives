BREAKING NEWS

The reference laboratory has now sequenced samples from selected sick students.

The students were chosen to represent different symptom patterns seen in the hospital records.

Compare the student samples with the reference organisms using the phylogenetic tree.

Questions to consider:
• Do all of the student samples cluster together?
• Which reference is closest to each group of student samples?
• Does the sequencing evidence support one outbreak, or could more than one illness be occurring?

IMPORTANT TEACHING NOTE:
The sequences in this activity are simulated teaching sequences. The tree deliberately compares very different organisms so that students can practise interpreting clustering. It is not a biologically valid phylogeny of viruses and parasites and must not be treated as real pathogen sequence data.

CONTACT TRACING UPDATE

Interviewers have completed contact tracing for the sick students.

Their records include contacts in classrooms, at lunch, during art class, at hockey training and around swimming practice.

Use the contact network to investigate how the cases are connected.

Questions to consider:
• Is there one connected group or more than one?
• Can you identify a large pattern consistent with person-to-person spread?
• Is there another group whose illness may be better explained by a shared exposure?

Remember: a contact network can suggest plausible transmission patterns, but it cannot prove exactly who infected whom.

Use the network together with the symptoms, locations and sequencing evidence.
